==================================================
MODULE PURPOSE
==================================================

UniLink should help students discover university clubs and communities based on their interests.

Students should be able to:

- Discover clubs
- Search clubs
- Browse by category
- View club details
- Join clubs
- Leave clubs
- See club events
- See club announcements
- See recruitment opportunities
- Save/follow clubs
- View their joined clubs

The experience should feel like a modern campus community platform combined with a professional SaaS application.

DESIGN QUALITY
==================================================

The final experience should feel like a production-ready modern campus SaaS platform.

Combine:

- Professional SaaS UI
- Campus community discovery
- Clean information hierarchy
- Subtle animations
- Personalization
- Strong usability

Maintain:

- Existing UniLink design system
- Existing components
- Existing sidebar
- Existing navigation
- Existing typography
- Existing colors
- Existing Light/Dark themes
- Existing animation language

Avoid:

- Traditional university portal aesthetics
- Excessive cards
- Excessive colors
- Heavy shadows
- Neon effects
- Excessive gradients
- Social-media-style clutter
- Gaming-style community interfaces
- Unnecessary engagement metrics

Do NOT add:

- Likes
- Followers
- Public social feeds
- Reactions
- Complex social media features

UniLink is a campus platform, not a social media clone.

The experience should communicate:

"Find your community. Discover your interests. Be part of campus life."
