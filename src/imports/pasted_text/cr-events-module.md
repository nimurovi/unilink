==================================================
PAGE PURPOSE
==================================================

Create a modern SaaS-style:

EVENTS

page for the Class Representative.

The CR Events page should help the CR discover, review, and share relevant opportunities with their assigned students.

The CR may share:

- University Events
- Department Events
- Club Events
- External Workshops
- Seminars
- Hackathons
- Competitions
- Career Events
- Training Programs

IMPORTANT:

Some workshops and events may be organized by organizations OUTSIDE the university.

External organizers may include:

- Technology companies
- Training organizations
- NGOs
- Startup communities
- Professional organizations
- Industry communities
- Independent workshop organizers

The CR should be able to share these opportunities with students.
